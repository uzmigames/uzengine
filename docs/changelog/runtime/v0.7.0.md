### PMREM Atlas → Cubemap Conversion
- **pmrem_to_cubemap_cs.hlsl**: compute shader extracting 6 faces × N LODs from Three.js CubeUV PMREM atlas
- **IBLBaker::PmremToCubemap()**: dispatches per-LOD extraction, copies into mipmapped cubemap (per-mip temp texture pattern)
- **IBLBaker::BakeIrradianceOnly()**: irradiance-only bake (64×64, skips expensive prefilter dispatch entirely)
- **SceneRenderer::SetPMREMEnvironment()**: end-to-end PMREM pipeline — extract cubemap, bake irradiance, SH projection, skybox wiring
- **frame_constants.cpp**: `isPMREM` branch calls `SetPMREMEnvironment()` instead of equirect fallback
- PMREM cubemap used directly as prefiltered specular (already prefiltered by Three.js PMREMGenerator)
- Eliminates redundant prefilter bake (was 1024×1024 × 8 mips × 4096 samples — caused GPU TDR)

### GBuffer Normal Precision Upgrade
- **RGBA8Unorm → RGBA16Float**: GBuffer Normal RT upgraded from 8-bit to half-float (10-bit mantissa)
- Angular resolution improved from ~0.7°/step to ~0.18°/step — eliminates visible stepping in specular reflections and AO on smooth curved surfaces
- All PSO descriptors updated: pipeline_geometry, pipeline_shadow (instanced), pipeline_vfx, material_pipeline
- Cost: +4 bytes/pixel (+8MB VRAM at 1080p) — negligible tradeoff for smooth reflections
