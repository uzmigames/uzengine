# Shader Improvement Analysis: VFX, Compute, IBL, and Utility Shaders

Deep shader-by-shader comparison of UzEngine vs industry references (Falcor, Niagara, Frostbite)
for VFX, compute utilities, IBL, and shared include shaders.

---

| 17 | `decal_deferred_ps.hlsl` | Normal blending | Blends two normals by `normalize(lerp(surfaceNormal, decalNormalWS, normalBlend))`. | Standard linear normal blending is not energy-conserving and produces visible "soft" normals at the blend boundary. | 18 | `distortion_ps.hlsl` | Distortion UV scale | `DISTORTION_UV_SCALE = 0.01f` is a hardcoded constant claimed to match "typical scale used in real-time VFX pipelines". | The constant `0.01` is declared as "empirically validated" but the comment says it maps a normal.xy of (1,0) at strength=1 to 0.01 UV offset (10 pixels at 1080p). | 19 | `distortion_resolve_ps.hlsl` | Warp UV clamping | `warpedUV = saturate(warpedUV)` clamps screen edges to the last valid pixel color, causing a color-bleed band at screen boundaries when distortion is large. | Replace `saturate` clamp with a gradual fade-to-zero of the distortion offset near screen edges. | 25 | `vfx_motion_vectors.hlsli` | Motion vector output | Outputs `currentUV - prevUV` in screen-space UV.
