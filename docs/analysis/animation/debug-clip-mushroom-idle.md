# Mushroom_Idle .uzanim Inspection Report

Temporary debug analysis. Generated by `scripts/inspect_uzanim.py`
(also temporary — delete after debugging).

## TL;DR — Smoking guns

1. **MushroomPelvis position deviates by 71.25 cm from bind on keyframe[0]**
   (bind = (0.00, 0.09, +78.38), key0 = (+2.70, +0.50, +7.18)). The pelvis is
   the chain root after `Point001` — moving it 71 cm down from its bind
   position will yank the entire mesh underground. Idle animations should
   start within ~1 cm of bind.
2. **Quaternion dot-products vs. bind are far below 0.99 on most bones**
   even at t=0:
   - `Point004`: dot = 0.2603 (≈75° angular delta — almost orthogonal)
   - `Point003`: dot = 0.0172 (≈88° — basically perpendicular)
   - `Point002`: dot = 0.5398, `Point005`: dot = 0.8986
   - `Bone009`: 0.8132, `Bone006`: 0.8163, `MushroomPelvis`: 0.8183
   These are NOT small idle deltas — they look like a different rotation
   *basis* between the clip and the skeleton.
3. **2 channels are not in the skeleton at all**: `MushroomRoot` and
   `Point001`. Either the importer is emitting clip channels for FBX nodes
   that were excluded from the skeleton, or the skeleton is missing two
   bones present in the FBX. If the runtime indexes channels by skeleton
   slot and these two are silently dropped, all subsequent bones might be
   evaluated against the *wrong* parent space.
4. **23 channels in the clip vs. 21 bones in the skeleton.**
   Off-by-2 matches the two extra bones in (3).
5. **The cyclic axis remap (x,y,z)→(z,x,y) on TRS** that was added
   yesterday is consistent with the magnitude of the rotation deltas seen.
   A cyclic axis swap rotates a quaternion's vector part by 120° about
   (1,1,1)/√3 — that produces dot products in roughly the 0.0–0.85 range
   exactly like Point003 (0.017), Point004 (0.260), MushroomPelvis (0.818).
   The pelvis Δp also looks like a swap: bind Z = 78.38, key0 Y = 0.50,
   key0 Z = 7.18 — i.e. the original Z-up translation was permuted into a
   different axis.

**Strongly suggests**: the (x,y,z)→(z,x,y) remap was applied to the clip
TRS but the skeleton bind transforms were either remapped differently
(or not at all). The two bases no longer agree, so as soon as the clip
overrides the bind pose, every bone evaluates in the wrong frame.

Recommended next step: re-inspect the .uzskel header / first three bones'
TRS, and compare to what's in the FBX before remap, to determine whether
the skeleton remap was skipped or applied with a different permutation.
