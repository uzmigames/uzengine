# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

UzEngine is a technical project. Participation — in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other channel — is expected to be professional, direct, and constructive.

We value:

- **Substance over status.** Good arguments stand on their merits, not on who makes them.
- **Directness without hostility.** Pointing out flaws in code or design is fine; attacking the person who wrote it is not.
- **Good faith.** Assume others are trying to help. Ask for clarification before assuming bad intent.
- **Proportionality.** Feedback should be calibrated to the severity of the issue — nitpicks stay small, critical bugs get critical attention.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following will result in action by maintainers without debate:

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory language directed at any person.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Deliberate intimidation or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing private information about someone without their consent.
- Sustained off-topic activity that degrades the usefulness of project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies everywhere this project operates: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussion boards, and any other forum hosted under this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will take action proportional to the violation:

1. **Warning** — a direct message explaining what violated the standard and what behavior is expected.
2. **Temporary block** — removal from project spaces for a defined period.
3. **Permanent ban** — for serious or repeated violations.

Enforcement decisions are made by maintainers and are final. If you witness a violation, report it by emailing the maintainers (see `README.md` for contact details). Reports are handled confidentially.

## For Maintainers

Maintainers are held to the same standard as contributors and have an additional obligation to model it. Maintainers who abuse their position will be removed.
