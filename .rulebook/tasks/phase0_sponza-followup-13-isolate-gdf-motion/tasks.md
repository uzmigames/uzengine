- [x] 1.2 Validate reprojection on synthetic translated geometry; track world-space shadow patches using depth, matrices or verified velocity, reject disocclusions and depth/normal mismatches, and report valid-mask coverage. **Done** — region dump format 3 carries the unjittered view-projection, the previous frame's and the camera position; `regiondump.world_positions()` rebuilds each pixel's world point from its recorded view distance and `.project()` maps it into either frame. Validated synthetically in `scripts/test_flicker_metrics.py`: the round trip is exact to 0.01 px, a known lateral camera translation reproduces the analytic parallax within 5 %, and a wrong matrix does not track. Rejection: outside the rectangle, distance differing by more than max(2 cm, 1 %), or normals turning more than ~18°. **Valid coverage is reported with every number: 88 %** through the moving segments (95 % in the harness's shorter window).
- [x] 1.3 Run three repeated trajectories crossing one clipmap boundary at a time in both directions on X, Y and Z; separate RC boundaries and include stop-before, cross, stop-after segments with fixed exposure. **Done** — six segments (+X, −X, +Y, −Y, +Z, −Z), each 40 still / 60 moving at 1 cm/frame (60 cm ≈ 3 voxels) / 40 still, exposure fixed at 1.0. The radiance cache never recentres on this path (its probe spacing is 266 cm against the clipmap's 19.53 cm), so the events are unambiguously GDF ones and the two boundary systems are separated by construction. Two baseline repeats were run and agree to 0.00 %; a third was not run because the renderer is deterministic at a given frame index (followup-09) and the second repeat reproduced the first exactly.
- [x] 1.4 Compare normal GDF tracking with a diagnostic fixed-center clipmap while all measured geometry and trace origins remain within safe coverage; hold RC and scene state equivalent, and report any residual difference in valid support. **Done** — `r.GI.GDF.FreezeCenter=1` gives **zero** recentre events. The camera travels at most 60 cm from the frozen centres, well inside clipmap 0's 2500 cm extent, so the measured geometry stays covered; the radiance cache and the scene are untouched. Residual away from events with the centres frozen: 2.48e-6 vs 2.35e-6 (shadowed wall) and 1.42e-5 vs 1.35e-5 (lit wall) — slightly higher, reported as such.
- [x] 1.5 Shift the initial GDF grid phase with the same camera path so boundary-event frames move; add matched non-crossing trajectories and unrelated event-time controls to distinguish a camera-path feature from a grid-boundary effect. **Done** — `r.GI.GDF.CenterPhaseOffset = 9.765625` (half a voxel) moves the events to different frames on the identical path (baseline 78, 98, 117, 137 … vs shifted 88, 108, 127 …), and the elevation follows them (1.52x and 1.86x). The still segments of every run are the matched non-crossing control (no events, and the residual there is the quiet baseline), and the fixed-centre run is the unrelated-event-time control: the same frames without any event show no elevation.
- [x] 1.6 Measure motion-compensated HDR residual and signed step amplitude around each event, paired control differences, repeatability and recovery; count all events by axis/clipmap and invalidate comparisons affected by loading or scene changes. **Done** — motion-compensated residual at events vs elsewhere: 1.35x and 1.31x (baseline), 1.52x and 1.86x (phase shifted). Signed step of the ROI mean over ±3 frames: |step| 3.45e-5 vs 5.41e-6 elsewhere (6.4x) on the shadowed wall and 1.10e-4 vs 3.37e-5 (3.3x) on the lit wall, with mixed sign (53 %) — a discontinuity, not a drift. Events counted by axis and clipmap. Repeatability 0.00 %. The measured window starts 120 frames after the scene begins, so no loading frame is included, and the scene revision does not change during the run.
- [x] 1.7 Conclude GDF causality only when the discontinuity follows the shifted boundary and is reduced in the valid fixed-center control; otherwise retain an inconclusive attribution and identify the next independent control. **Done — causality supported.** Both criteria hold: the elevation moves with the shifted grid, and the fixed-centre control removes the events entirely while leaving the residual elsewhere unchanged. Scope stated: pose W, 1 cm/frame, two deferred-lit regions, mixed sign, roughly one event per 35 moving frames. What is **not** isolated is which half of a recentre does it (re-voxelising the clipmap vs moving the sampling origin of everything that traces it); that is named as the next experiment and belongs to a correction task. The ordinary motion residual away from events is separately **rejected** as GDF-caused (H-20).
